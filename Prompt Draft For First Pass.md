Build "Umroo's Music Mixer" - An Ai playlist maker, an ai that knows your taste. Create mixes of music you are familiar with, mix it with new music or explore something entirely new to you. Built for everyone.

Umroo's Music Mixer knows it's users taste and can create a playlist exactly for them. The user enters a prompt for what vibe they are looking for or how they want the playlist to be and the music mixer makes it exactly for you. Want to explore something new? Want to explore something new with a touch of familiar artists? no problem. 

Here are examples of prompts users might use and the app needs to support for sure.

1. Artist / song oriented prompts referring to a specific artist / album / song. 
   "Create a house music playlist with mainly drake's house music with occasional songs from other artists I may not have heard before"
2. Prompts referring to their own taste. 
   "Create a playlist of artist I have heard to most recently." OR "Avoiding songs that I have heard a lot recently,  dig up the old gems I used to listen to that might have been forgotten"
3. Mood Oriented prompts.
   "Create a playlist with a soft indie vibe like yel's music and her recent song GHOST"
4. Quantity mentions in prompts should also be respected to a limit. A playlist should contain a minimum of 15 songs, even if the user says lower it doesn't matter thats the lowest it should go. A maximum of 200 songs should be supported again, no matter what the user mentions. For normal playlists with no defined limit prefer the range between 30 to 50 songs.
   "Create a house music playlist with mainly drake's house music with occasional songs from other artists I may not have heard before add atleast 60 songs"
5. Situation / genre based prompts.
   "I just woke up. Im in the sleepy and relaxed zone I want an R&B Music playlist with slow songs. Nothing too hype or loud / energetic. Add the usual artists I like, Keep the playlist Drake heavy while also including some Sza music in between."

The Goal is to adapt to the user's request with pristine prompt understanding and creating a playlist with the exact vibe the user mentioned in their prompt / instructions. The Ai should assume the least effort / detail from the user and not assume things based off of the prompts, even short prompts like "give me a short and sweet fun playlist with a mix of genres" should adapt perfectly creating something with the user's taste preferred and a mix of artists and genres they would listen to usually. This setting should be default (User familiar taste with occasional new songs the user hasn't explored yet that they might like ) unless explicitly mentioned by the user.
